# Product Promo (`product-promo`)

Benefit-driven three-act promo (`Problem -> Proof -> CTA`) engineered with a `<= 2.0 s` opening hook, asset SPDX license tracking, and verified claim provenance.

## Structural contract

1. **Problem hook (`<= 60` frames at `30 fps`)** — Opens immediately on frame `0` with `prism-monolith` depth and a `sub_impact` transient cue (`opening-hook`).
2. **Proof beat (`PROOF` / `KANIT` / `BELEG`)** — Renders supplied product photography (`layer-stack` auto-registered as `User-Provided` in `.reelsyntax/asset-licenses.json`) or deterministic `data-horizon` benefit bars.
3. **CTA end card (`START NOW` / `HEMEN` / `JETZT`)** — Crisp closing action card held cleanly inside safe bounds.

## Wired skills

- `product-commercial` — Problem, proof, and end-card commercial cadence.
- `opening-hook` — Enforces `<= 2.0 s` immediate opening hook.
- `claims-must-be-sourced` — Requires `sources.json` or brief-derived provenance for numeric claims.
- `asset-license-ledger` — Tracks SHA-256 + SPDX provenance for user or starter product media.
- `contrast-audit` — Verifies WCAG typography contrast ratios.
- `frame-clock` — Exact integer frame distribution with hook-cap conservation.
