# Reduced motion variant

Prepare a lower-motion variant of an edit.

1. Inventory camera shifts, repeating motion and full-frame zooms.
2. Replace each with a hold or short dissolve while preserving the narrative cue.
3. Compare durations and read all labels at playback speed.
