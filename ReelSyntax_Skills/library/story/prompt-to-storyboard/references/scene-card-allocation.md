## Field separation contract
- `kicker`: `1–2` uppercase category/step tokens (`STEP 02`, `ADIM 02`, `SCHRITT 02`).
- `heading`: `2–4` high-impact words; never end with a period unless stylized intentionally.
- `subheading`: single supporting clause (`<= 52` chars vertical, `<= 72` chars horizontal).
- `narration`: natural spoken sentence expanding the mechanism rather than reading `kicker + heading` robotically.
